# Advisors

## Academic Advisor

### Role
Not a sponsor or figurehead. A quarterly check-in partner who reviews community direction against SC rigour.

Specifically:
- Read the last 2 case write-ups before each check-in
- Tell us if sessions are drifting from SC focus
- Flag if the community is becoming too generic or too social
- Optional: one guest appearance per year at a roundtable or AMA

### Time Commitment
30 minutes, once per quarter. Nothing more unless they choose it.

### How to Pitch This to a Professor
*"I'm building a practitioner-first supply chain community starting with our IMBA cohort. I need someone to tell me if we lose SC rigour — that's the only ask. 30 minutes every three months. Would you be willing to be our academic advisor?"*

### Target Profile
IE professor with supply chain, operations, or procurement focus. Someone who has seen students drift into generic networking communities and knows how to prevent it.

---

## Industry Advisor

### Role
Provides market-level signal that the academic environment cannot.

Specifically:
- Share what is actually happening in SC operations right now
- Help identify relevant external speakers for AMAs
- Lend credibility when approaching companies for partnerships or event support
- Optional: bring a real problem to one roundtable per year

### Time Commitment
30 minutes, once per quarter. Async input when needed.

### Target Profile
Senior SC practitioner — Director or VP level in procurement, logistics, or operations at a company relevant to the community's focus.

---

## Quarterly Review Questions

These are asked at every advisor check-in:

1. Based on what we've published, are we still genuinely focused on supply chain?
2. Is there a topic or format we should be doing that we are not?
3. Is there a topic or format we are doing that we should drop?
4. Who should we be talking to that we are not?
