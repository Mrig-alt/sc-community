# Community Charter

## Domain

Global supply chain — including procurement, logistics, manufacturing operations, demand planning, inventory management, supplier relationship management, trade and customs, supply chain analytics, and operations transformation.

## Purpose

To create a structured, practitioner-first space where supply chain professionals diagnose real problems, share honest experience, and build genuine expertise through peer exchange.

## Who Belongs Here

- Practitioners with supply chain or operations experience
- MBA students targeting supply chain, operations, or consulting roles with SC exposure
- Consultants working on SC and operations engagements
- Founders and operators building supply chain businesses or tools
- People from adjacent fields (data, finance, tech) with direct SC problem exposure

## What Is In Scope

- Real supply chain problems from members' work or internships
- Frameworks, models, and tools that practitioners actually use
- Case studies from real companies — diagnosing what went wrong or right
- Industry signals — disruptions, trade shifts, structural changes
- Career insight — how SC roles work, how companies think, what skills matter

## What Is Out of Scope

- General business strategy not tied to SC
- Motivational or inspirational content
- Job postings (members may share in designated threads only)
- Generic MBA networking
- Certification prep

## The Drift Test

Every month, ask: *"Would a supply chain manager with five years of experience find this session genuinely useful?"*

If the answer is no for two consecutive months, the community has drifted. That is the signal to review with an advisor and recalibrate.

## Chapter Principles

1. Every chapter runs at least one event per quarter
2. Every session produces a written output
3. Chapter leads are active participants, not administrators
4. No chapter grows faster than its ability to maintain quality
5. All chapters share the same formats, the same values, and the same GitHub repository

## Governance

- Founder: overall direction, quality, and community health
- Chapter leads: local activation, event execution, member curation
- Advisors: quarterly check-in, drift review, external signal
- Members: bring problems, contribute outputs, hold the community to its standard
