# Code of Conduct

## The Standard

This community exists for supply chain practitioners to work through real problems together. That only works if every member holds the space to a high standard.

## Expected Behaviour

- Engage with intellectual honesty — say what you actually think
- Bring real problems, not hypotheticals designed to impress
- Credit others when you use their ideas or frameworks
- Be direct, be kind — both are possible at the same time
- Respect that members share professional challenges here that they may not share elsewhere

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Recruiting-first behaviour — using community access primarily to source candidates or clients
- Spam — unsolicited promotions, irrelevant links, self-promotion without contribution
- Dismissiveness — treating practitioner experience as less valuable than academic credentials
- Harassment or exclusion of any kind

## Reporting

Raise concerns directly with the founder or chapter lead. Issues will be handled privately and promptly.
