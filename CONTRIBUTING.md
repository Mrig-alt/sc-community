# Contributing

This community is built on the principle that real operators work on real problems together. Contributing means bringing genuine supply chain thinking — not just showing up.

This document tells you how to plug in depending on what you want to contribute.

---

## Two Ways to Contribute

### 1. Community contributions — start on Telegram

If you want to:
- Attend a session
- Bring a real SC problem to a Deep Dive
- Connect someone relevant to the community
- Give feedback on a case write-up
- Suggest a session topic or guest

**Start on Telegram.** Message the community or the founder directly. No formal process — just a real conversation first.

Telegram is the human entry point. It is where you introduce yourself, say what you do, and tell us what you want to work on.

---

### 2. Project contributions — start on GitHub

If you want to:
- Contribute to the World Supply Chain Chokepoint Map ([Issue #11](https://github.com/Mrig-alt/sc-community/issues/11))
- Help with research, data sourcing, or analysis
- Write or improve a case write-up
- Suggest or improve a community document

**Start on GitHub.** Look at the open issues, pick one that matches your skills, and comment on it to say you want to work on it. The founder will respond within a few days.

GitHub is the structured entry point. It is where work is tracked, reviewed, and published.

---

## What a Good First Contribution Looks Like

**Good:**
- You attend a session and send a follow-up message with one thing you want to add to the write-up
- You find a free data source for Issue #11 that is not already in the list and comment on the issue with the link and what it covers
- You bring a real SC problem from your work to a Deep Dive session
- You read a case write-up and flag one thing that is factually wrong or missing

**Not good:**
- Vague offers to "help with anything"
- Suggesting partnerships or sponsorships before attending a session
- Adding to the repo without discussing it first
- Bringing consultancy pitches into session discussions

---

## Contribution Standards

- Everything published under this community reflects SC rigour — if it is not grounded in real operations, it does not get published
- Write-ups are based on real sessions with real problems — they are not thought leadership pieces
- Data contributions to GitHub projects must cite their source
- If you are unsure whether something is a good contribution, ask on Telegram first

---

## Who Is Welcome

- SC practitioners with real operational experience
- MBA students with SC focus or background
- Researchers working on SC-adjacent problems
- Professors or industry advisors by invitation

This is not a general business networking community. The supply chain focus is the filter.

---

## Questions

Telegram first for anything human. GitHub issues for anything structural. If in doubt, Telegram.
