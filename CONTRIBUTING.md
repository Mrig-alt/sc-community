# Contributing

This repository is the operating system for the Madrid–Mumbai SC Community.

## Who Can Contribute

- Founder
- Chapter leads
- Named stewards (newsletter, events, content, frameworks)
- Community members assigned to specific folders

## How to Contribute

**Small updates** (fixing typos, updating status) — commit directly to main.

**New content** (case write-ups, new frameworks, event summaries) — add files to the relevant folder, update the status file.

**Proposing ideas** — open a GitHub Issue.

**Event ideas**, **partner leads**, **chapter updates** — open an issue with relevant context.

## What Not to Do

- Do not commit personal phone numbers, private email addresses, or CVs
- Do not upload confidential company information or case details without anonymisation
- Do not change the CHARTER or VISION without founder review
