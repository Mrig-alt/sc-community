# Event Formats

## 1. SC Deep Dive (Monthly Roundtable)

**Size**: 12–20 people  
**Duration**: 60–75 minutes  
**Output**: 1-page case write-up published on GitHub and LinkedIn

### Format
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0–5 min | Welcome and context — what problem are we solving today |
| 5–10 min | Intros: name, SC background, one word for what you want from this session |
| 10–40 min | The case — one member presents a real SC problem, group diagnoses |
| 40–50 min | Synthesis — what did we learn, what would we do differently |
| 50–60 min | Wrap — next session date, any new responsibilities assigned |

### Rules
- One person brings a real problem every session
- No abstract discussions — everything must connect to the case
- The write-up is published within 48 hours

---

## 2. SC Sprint (Quarterly Build Session)

**Size**: 20–30 people  
**Duration**: 2–3 hours  
**Output**: Prototype, solution sketch, or decision framework built in the session

### Format
- Teams of 4–5 people (mixed SC + tech backgrounds)
- One real supply chain problem per team
- Teams use tools (Lovable, Claude, Sheets, whatever works) to build a working prototype or solution in 2 hours
- Final 30 minutes: each team presents, community votes on most useful solution

### Partner Logic
Co-hosted with a tech community (Pitchless model). SC community brings the domain, tech community brings the builders.

---

## 3. SC Connector (Curated Mixer)

**Size**: 10–15 people  
**Duration**: 90 minutes  
**Output**: Each attendee leaves with 2–3 specific warm intros

### Format
- Applications-only or invite-only
- Founder or chapter lead reviews background of every attendee before the event
- Each person gets a card with 2–3 names and why they should talk
- First 30 minutes: structured intros using the card
- Remaining time: open conversation

### Why This Is Rare
Most mixers are random. This one is curated. That is the differentiator.

---

## 4. AMA / Operator Session (From Month 3)

**Size**: Open  
**Duration**: 45–60 minutes  
**Output**: Summary notes published

### Format
- One external SC practitioner or operator
- Community submits questions in advance via Telegram bot
- Questions ranked by upvote
- Live session works through top questions
- Summary notes published on GitHub and newsletter

### Speaker Topics
- Procurement strategy and supplier risk
- Last-mile logistics
- Operations transformation
- Demand planning and S&OP
- Supply chain analytics and visibility tools
- Manufacturing SC in India, LATAM, or Southeast Asia
