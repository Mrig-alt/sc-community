## Phase 1 — Seeding *(starts after Phase 0)*

**Gate: 15–20 people confirmed for founding session.**

No LinkedIn posts. No public announcements. Seeding happens through warm, direct 1:1 outreach to known SC contacts in the IE IMBA cohort.

| Action | Output |
|---|---|
| Profile IE cohort for SC relevance | Tier A / B / C tagging complete |
| Send warm DMs to Tier A contacts | 8–12 conversations started |
| Identify case-bringer for founding session | One person with a real SC problem confirmed |
| Send founding session invite | 15–20 confirmed attendees |
| Decide community name | Name confirmed before any public material goes out |

**What Phase 1 complete looks like:**
15–20 people have said yes to the founding session. A case-bringer is confirmed. The community has a name.
