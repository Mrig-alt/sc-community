# Partnerships

## Principles

1. Borrow reach, never identity — partner for one event, not for our community infrastructure
2. Every partner must add something we cannot do alone — audience, venue, brand, tools
3. We stay SC-specific — no partner relationship should pull us into general tech or business content
4. Partners get credit, not control

---

## Tech Community Partnership (Pitchless / BETA DASH model)

### What They Bring
- Established Madrid builder audience (110+ members)
- Event infrastructure and venue relationships
- Credibility in the startup and product community

### What We Bring
- Supply chain domain expertise
- IE cohort network
- Real SC problems to work on

### Event Format
SC Sprint — cross-functional teams work on a real SC problem using Lovable or similar tools. SC community provides the domain; tech community provides the builders.

### Rules
- Co-host one event, not ongoing infrastructure
- Our SC members come first in invitations
- We publish the write-up on our GitHub, not on their platform
- We maintain our own identity throughout

---

## Platform Partner (Lovable Model)

### Logic
Find one SC-adjacent software company that wants practitioner exposure.

What they provide: brand on the event, potentially credits or access to their tool, promotion through their channels.

What we provide: access to SC practitioners who are potential future customers and advocates.

### Target Partners
- Supply chain visibility tools (project44, Flexport, Coupa)
- Procurement platforms
- Demand planning tools
- SC analytics companies

### Pitch Frame
*"We run practitioner-led SC events. Every attendee is a current or future SC decision-maker. We want your brand on the event and your tool in the room. You want access to this audience. Let's make one event together and see how it goes."*

## Academic and Career Office Partnerships

- IE Career Services — co-promote events to students, share job and internship leads
- Future: LBS, ISB, Ross, ESADE — guest chapter events, cross-cohort access

---

## What We Do Not Do

- No paid sponsorships until 200+ active members
- No logo-on-everything arrangements
- No partners who want to control event content
- No partnerships that require us to leave our SC focus
