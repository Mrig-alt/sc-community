# Pitch Story: The Antigravity Edge

## The Hook
The supply chain world is drowning in data but starving for practitioner intelligence.

## The Problem
ERP systems and academic journals are too slow for real-time disruption. Professionals rely on informal, fragmented networks.

## The Solution
A tech-enabled, open-source community that turns practitioner chatter into structured, global intelligence.

## The Team/Traction
- **Leadership**: MBA + Logistics expertise + Automation background.
- **Traction**: India and Madrid chapters established.
- **Tech**: Sentinel Bot architecture ready for scale.
