# Madrid–Mumbai Supply Chain Community

> *Most supply chain communities share articles. We solve problems.*

A practitioner-first, chapter-based global supply chain community — built for people who work in, study, or are building careers in supply chain, operations, procurement, and logistics.

We run structured roundtables, cross-functional build sprints, and curated connector sessions. Every session produces something real: a case write-up, a framework, a tool, or a decision.

---

## Why This Exists

Supply chain knowledge is fragmented. Practitioners in FMCG, pharma, logistics, consulting, and manufacturing rarely talk to each other across sectors or geographies. Most SC communities are either too broad, too passive, or too focused on certifications. There is no modern, informal, practitioner-led space where real problems get diagnosed and real outputs get produced.

This community exists to fill that gap.

---

## Why Madrid and Mumbai

These are not arbitrary cities.

**Madrid** — Home to a dense international MBA cohort (IE Business School), strong European procurement and logistics networks, and adjacent to Spain's active positioning as India's EU supply chain gateway.

**Mumbai** — India's industrial and commercial capital. Home to major FMCG, pharma, logistics, and manufacturing operations. The city where the India chapter is anchored.

Together, Madrid and Mumbai sit on one of the most watched supply chain corridors globally: the India–Europe trade axis accelerated by China+1 strategies and EU-India trade negotiations.
