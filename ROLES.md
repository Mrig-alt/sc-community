# Roles

This document defines who owns what. It exists so that when a new person joins, there is no ambiguity about lanes.

Current state: **solo founder operation.** One person owns everything. This document also defines what the first additions look like and when they come in.

---

## Founder (Current — Rahul Yadav)

Owns everything until Phase 3 (50 members). Specific responsibilities:

**Strategy and direction**
- Community concept, positioning, and name decision
- Event format decisions
- Advisor and partner relationships
- Repo structure and documentation

**Seeding and outreach**
- 1:1 DMs to IE cohort
- Founding session invite and curation
- Case-bringer identification

**Events**
- Session hosting and facilitation
- Post-session follow-up
- Case write-up review and publishing

**Operations**
- Telegram group setup and early management
- GitHub issues and project tracking
- Monthly status updates in [updates/](./updates/)

---

## AI Agent — Content and Write-Ups

Content production and write-ups are handled by an AI agent, not a human hire. This covers:

- First draft of case write-ups from session notes
- LinkedIn post drafts
- Newsletter drafts
- Session summaries for Telegram

Founder reviews, edits, and publishes. The agent produces, the founder decides.

This is not a role that will be replaced by a human in Phase 1 or Phase 2.

## Role 1 — Community and Member Operations *(hire when proof exists)*

**When this role is added:** after the founding session is complete and there is at least one published output to show. Not before.

**What this role owns:**
- Day-to-day Telegram management — welcoming new members, moderating discussion, keeping signal-to-noise high
- Member tracking — updating [operations/MEMBER-TRACKER.md](./operations/MEMBER-TRACKER.md)
- Event logistics support — room booking, invite management, attendance tracking
- Outreach support — helping identify and warm up new contacts for seeding

**What this role does not own:**
- Community direction or strategy
- Content or write-ups (AI agent)
- Advisor and partner relationships (founder)
- GitHub repo and documentation (founder)

**Profile:**
- SC background or strong interest
- Organised, reliable, low-ego
- Can operate with minimal direction once lanes are clear
- Madrid-based preferred for Phase 1

## Role Addition Rule

No one is added to the team until:
1. The founding session has happened
2. At least one published output exists (case write-up on GitHub)
3. The repo is clean enough that a new person can read it and know what to do

Adding people before this creates confusion, not capacity.
