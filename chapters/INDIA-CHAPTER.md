# India Chapter — Mumbai

## Why Mumbai

Mumbai is India's commercial and industrial capital. It is home to the headquarters of major FMCG companies (HUL, P&G, Nestlé India), pharmaceutical manufacturers (Sun Pharma, Cipla), logistics operators (DP World India, Maersk India), and the financial institutions that fund supply chain infrastructure across South Asia.

Beyond the companies, Mumbai has a dense network of mid-career professionals in SC-adjacent roles — distribution, channel management, procurement, warehouse operations — who rarely have a structured space to share what they know.

## Why India–Madrid

Spain and India have an active bilateral supply chain relationship. Spain is explicitly positioned as a key EU entry point for Indian trade and industrial exports. The India–Europe SC corridor is one of the most watched shifts in global trade as companies diversify away from China.

Running chapters in both cities is not symbolic. It reflects the actual flow of goods, investment, and talent between these markets.

## What the India Chapter Does

- Hosts at least one SC event per quarter in Mumbai
- Surfaces India-specific SC problems and case material for the global community
- Identifies Mumbai-based practitioners, speakers, and potential advisors
- Manages the India WhatsApp coordination circle
- Contributes to the GitHub repo — updates this file, writes India case summaries, maintains a local member list

## What the Co-Lead Owns

The India chapter co-lead is not an assistant. They are the chapter operator.

Specifically:
- Identify 10–15 Mumbai-based people who should be in this community
- Scout and confirm a venue for the first Mumbai session
- Co-host or host the first India event
- Keep the Mumbai WhatsApp group active and purposeful
- Feed back signals from the Mumbai market — what SC challenges are real right now

- Building tech or automation
- Managing LinkedIn or GitHub independently in the early phase
- Committing large amounts of time before the community has proven its value

## India SC Context — What Makes Mumbai Relevant

- **FMCG**: One of the most complex SC environments globally — seasonal demand, last-mile complexity, vast rural distribution
- **Pharma**: Stringent cold chain, regulatory compliance, export-driven manufacturing
- **Logistics**: DP World, Maersk, DHL all have major India operations headquartered in or near Mumbai
- **GST impact**: India's tax reform fundamentally changed how supply chains are structured domestically
- **PLI scheme**: Production-Linked Incentive schemes are reshaping manufacturing supply chains across electronics, pharma, textiles
- **China+1**: India is the primary beneficiary of global diversification away from Chinese manufacturing

These are not abstract trends. They are live, real challenges for practitioners in Mumbai right now.
