# Madrid Chapter

## Anchor

IE Business School, Madrid — international MBA cohort with members from 40+ countries, many with SC, operations, consulting, and logistics backgrounds.

## Why Madrid

- Dense concentration of globally mobile MBA students with SC-relevant backgrounds
- Access to European procurement networks, logistics hubs, and consulting offices
- Proximity to Spain's role as India's EU supply chain gateway
- Adjacent tech and startup community (Pitchless, BETA DASH, Madrid startup ecosystem)

## What the Madrid Chapter Does

- Hosts the monthly SC Deep Dive (core format)
- Runs founding sessions for the global community
- Manages the Telegram community hub
- Leads LinkedIn content creation
- Coordinates tech community partnerships (buildathons, joint events)
- Maintains the GitHub repository

## Partner Ecosystem

**Pitchless / BETA DASH**: Madrid-based builder community running buildathons and mixers. Natural co-host for a cross-functional SC Sprint event.

**Lovable**: Platform partner for SC Sprint events — teams build supply chain tools using Lovable in 2-hour sessions.

**IE Career Services**: Institutional link for events, speaker access, and student recruitment.

## First Events

1. Founding session — 15–20 IE cohort members, one real SC case, one write-up output
2. SC Sprint collab — co-hosted with Pitchless, Lovable-powered, cross-functional teams
3. SC Connector — small curated mixer, 10–12 people, structured intros
