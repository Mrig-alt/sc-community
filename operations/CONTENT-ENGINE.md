## LinkedIn Content Plan

### First 5 Posts (in order)

1. **Observation post** — "6 months into my MBA, I've met SC professionals from 30+ countries. None of their knowledge is being captured anywhere." — Founding thesis, authentically told.

2. **Framework post** — Kraljic Matrix explained in plain language. What it is, when to use it, what most people get wrong.

3. **Signal post** — One sharp take on a current SC disruption (tariffs, Red Sea, nearshoring, China+1). What it means for practitioners.

4. **Case breakdown** — One famous SC failure or win (Boeing, Nike, Toyota). What happened, what the diagnosis is, what practitioners can learn.

5. **Launch post** — What I am building, why, and who it is for. Only after Posts 1–4 are live and have engagement.

### Post Format Rules
- No motivational content
- Always include one concrete example
- End with one question to the reader
- Keep under 300 words for observation posts, under 500 for frameworks
- Never use "the backbone of the global economy" or similar phrases

## Newsletter Structure (Monthly)

Each issue follows this structure:

1. **Signal** — One SC disruption or trend worth watching this month
2. **Framework** — One practical SC tool or model explained in 200 words
3. **Case Recap** — Summary of the most recent Deep Dive session
4. **Member Insight** — One quote or observation from a member (anonymous if needed)
5. **What's Next** — Upcoming events and open questions

---

## Event Recap Format

After every session, publish a write-up within 48 hours:

```
[X] people. [Y] countries. [One-sentence description of problem].

The Problem: [2–3 sentences]
What We Found: [3–5 bullet points]
The Framework We Used: [Name and one-line description]
What We Would Do Differently: [1–2 sentences]
Next Session: [Date and format]
```

Publish to GitHub under `/updates/` and post a summary on LinkedIn.
