# Content Engine: Community Intelligence

## Objective
To transform raw community discussion into structured, actionable intelligence.

## Workflow
1. **Input**: Telegram discussions, member submissions, news scrapers.
2. **Process**: Weekly synthesis of the "Top 3 Disruptions" and "Top 3 Solutions".
3. **Output**: Friday "Intelligence Brief" sent via Telegram and archived on GitHub.

## Content Pillars
- **Toolbox**: Reviews of supply chain software and automation hacks.
- **The Trench**: Case studies of real-world disruption management.
- **Future-Proof**: Deep dives into quantum-ready logistics and AI.
