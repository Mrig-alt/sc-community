# Events Playbook

## Format Types
- **Flash Talks**: 15-minute high-impact virtual sessions on a specific tool or tactic.
- **Deep-Dives**: 90-minute workshop sessions with Q&A.
- **The "Walkthrough"**: Physical tours of supply chain infrastructure.

## Planning Checklist
- [ ] Define objective and audience.
- [ ] Secure speaker and tech setup.
- [ ] Announce 2 weeks in advance via Telegram/LinkedIn.
- [ ] Record and archive highlights.
