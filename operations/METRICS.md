# Community Metrics

A lightweight framework for tracking community health without over-engineering it.

## Core KPIs

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Active Member Rate** | % of Telegram members who engage (reply, react, or post) in any given week | >40% monthly |
| **Session Attendance Rate** | % of invited members who attend each session | >60% per session |
| **Output Velocity** | Number of case write-ups, frameworks, or resources published per month | 1 per session minimum |
| **Membership Growth** | Net new members added per month | Organic — quality over speed |
| **Chapter Health** | At least one event per chapter per quarter | 1 per quarter per chapter |

## What We Don't Track

- Vanity metrics: follower counts, LinkedIn impressions, total group size
- Activity for activity's sake: message volume without substance
- Growth rate as a goal: the community should grow as a byproduct of quality, not as a target

## Reporting Rhythm

- **Monthly**: Founder reviews the 5 KPIs above privately
- **Quarterly**: Short update posted to Telegram group (2-3 sentences, honest)
- **Archived**: Each quarterly update saved to `/updates/` on GitHub

## Health Signal

The single best indicator of community health is this: *Are members bringing real problems to sessions, and are they coming back?*

If yes — the metrics will follow.
If no — no metric will fix it.
