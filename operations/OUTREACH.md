# Outreach & Growth Strategy

## Target Channels
- **LinkedIn**: Thought leadership posts tagging the community GitHub.
- **Industry Newsletters**: PR for major community milestones.
- **University Partnerships**: Recruiting the next generation of practitioners.

## Tactics
- **The "Source of Truth" Hook**: Directing professionals to our open-source standards.
- **Referral Loop**: Encouraging current members to invite one peer per quarter.
- **Guest Speakers**: Inviting industry veterans to "Practitioner Spotlight" sessions.
