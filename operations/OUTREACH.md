# Outreach

## Sequence

Do not broadcast before doing this in order:

1. **Discovery DMs** — 5–8 people you already know have SC backgrounds. Not a pitch. Just: "I've been thinking about building something for SC practitioners in our cohort. Would love 15 minutes to hear your take."

2. **1:1 coffees/calls with Tier A** — Short conversations. Three questions only:
   - What knowledge gap does the MBA not fill for SC?
   - What would make you keep coming back to a community like this?
   - Do you have a real SC problem you'd bring to a first session?

3. **Formal founding session invite** — Only after 1:1s. Frame it as a "working session" not a "networking event."

4. **Broadcast to broader cohort** — Only after the first session has happened and produced something real.

---

## Member Tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| A | Direct SC/ops/procurement/logistics/manufacturing background | Personal 1:1 invite |
| B | Consulting, tech, or finance with SC-adjacent work | Group invite |
| C | No SC relevance | Leave for now |

## Outreach Messages

### Discovery DM
> "Hey [name] — I've been thinking about building something for SC and ops practitioners in our cohort. Nothing formal yet — just a monthly working session where one person brings a real problem and we work through it together. Would love 15 minutes to hear your take before I go further with it. Up for a quick call this week?"

### Founding Session Invite
> "Hey [name] — I'm putting together a small founding session for a supply chain community I'm building with a few people from the cohort. The idea is simple: one person brings a real SC problem, we diagnose it together, and we publish a one-page write-up. First session is [date]. Would you want to join as one of the founding members?"

### Professor Advisor Pitch
> "Professor [name] — I'm building a practitioner-first supply chain community starting with our IMBA cohort and expanding to Madrid and Mumbai. I'm not looking for active involvement — just someone who can do a 30-minute check-in once a quarter to tell me if we're losing SC rigour. Would you be open to being our academic advisor?"

### India Co-Lead Pitch (for Satya)
> "Satya — I'm building a global supply chain community, anchored in Madrid and Mumbai. I don't need you to be an SC expert. I need someone in Mumbai I trust who can run local events, open connections, and help me make the India chapter real. Your network and your BD instincts are exactly what I need. Would you be the Mumbai Chapter Lead?"
