# Partnerships Framework

## Principles
- **No Pay-to-Play**: Partners must provide tangible value to practitioners (tools, data, access).
- **Transparency**: All sponsorship or partnership terms are disclosed.

## Categories
1. **Tech Partners**: Software vendors providing community trials or discounts.
2. **Knowledge Partners**: Research firms sharing whitepapers.
3. **Logistics Partners**: Physical infrastructure providers (Ports, Warehouses) offering tours or data access.
