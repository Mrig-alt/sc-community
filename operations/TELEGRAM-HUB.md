# Telegram Hub Architecture

## Channel Structure
- **#Main**: General announcements and community intelligence briefs.
- **#Discussion**: Peer-to-peer chat for verified practitioners.
- **#Alerts**: Real-time disruption signals.
- **#Chapters**: Sub-folders for India, Madrid, etc.

## Bot Integration
- **WelcomeBot**: Verification and rule-signing.
- **SignalBot**: Scrapes discussions for keywords (e.g., "port strike", "shortage").
- **DigestBot**: Summarizes the day's top discussions.
