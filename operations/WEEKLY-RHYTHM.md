# Weekly Rhythm

This is the solo founder operating cadence. It defines what actually happens each week — not aspirationally, but as a minimum viable operating week.

When Role 1 (Community and Member Operations) joins, this document will be updated to reflect a shared cadence.

---

## The Minimum Viable Week

These are the things that must happen every week, even if everything else slips:

1. **One SC signal or insight posted to Telegram** — keeps the group alive and sets the intellectual tone
2. **One outreach action** — a DM, a follow-up, or a conversation moved forward
3. **One repo action** — an update, a commit, a decision logged

If these three things happen, the week was not wasted.

---

## Weekly Cadence

### Monday — Orient
- Review the week ahead: any events, deadlines, or decisions pending?
- Check [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) — has anything unblocked?
- Scan SC news for the week's Telegram signal topic

### Tuesday — Outreach
- Send or follow up on 1–2 DMs to IE cohort or warm contacts
- Log any responses or conversations in [operations/MEMBER-TRACKER.md](./MEMBER-TRACKER.md)

### Wednesday — Content
- Draft or review one piece of content: Telegram signal, LinkedIn post draft, or session write-up section
- Hand off to AI agent for first draft if needed

### Thursday — Operations
- GitHub: update any open issues, log decisions, push any pending repo changes
- Review [operations/METRICS.md](./METRICS.md) — is anything worth noting this week?

### Friday — Publish and Review
- Post the week's Telegram signal
- Review the week: did the 3 minimum viable actions happen?
- Note anything that needs to carry into next week

---

## Event Weeks

In a week where a session is happening:

- **Monday** — confirm attendees, send reminder, confirm case-bringer is ready
- **Day of session** — host, take notes, capture key decisions and insights
- **Day after session** — send follow-up to attendees, open case write-up draft in GitHub
- **Within 5 days** — case write-up published on GitHub, summary posted to Telegram

---

## Weekly Review Questions

Ask these every Friday:

1. Did the 3 minimum viable actions happen?
2. Did any decision get made that needs to be logged in [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)?
3. Is there anything that has been sitting undone for more than 2 weeks? If yes — do it, delegate it, or drop it.
4. What is the single most important thing for next week?
