# SC Reading List

Curated by the community. Updated after each session.

---

## Essential Books

**The Goal — Eliyahu Goldratt**
The foundational novel on Theory of Constraints. Reads like fiction, teaches operations. Required reading.

**The Machine That Changed the World — Womack, Jones, Roos**
The definitive account of the Toyota Production System and lean manufacturing. Foundational.

**Supply Chain Management — Sunil Chopra and Peter Meindl**
The standard academic text. Dense but comprehensive. Use as reference, not cover-to-cover.

**Demand-Driven Material Requirements Planning — Carol Ptak and Chad Smith**
Modern take on inventory and production planning. Relevant for anyone working in manufacturing SC.

## Newsletters and Reports

- **Supply Chain Dive** — daily SC news, company-level updates
- **McKinsey Supply Chain Insights** — strategic takes, worth reading quarterly
- **Gartner Supply Chain Top 25** — annual benchmark of leading SC organisations
- **ASCM Insights** — practitioner-oriented association content

---

## Podcasts

- **The Logistics of Logistics** — practitioner interviews, specific and detailed
- **Supply Chain Now** — broad coverage, good for keeping current
- **Invest Like the Best (SC episodes)** — investor lens on SC businesses, useful for strategic thinking
