# Community Charter: Project Antigravity

## 1. Mission Statement
To empower supply chain practitioners through a tech-enabled, peer-to-peer intelligence network that prioritizes real-world execution over theoretical discussion.

## 2. Core Values
- **Practitioner-First**: We value insights from those in the trenches.
- **Transparency**: All community processes and standards are open-source.
- **Action-Oriented**: Focus on solutions to disruptions, not just identifying them.
- **Mutual Growth**: When one practitioner succeeds, the entire network gains intelligence.

## 3. Governance
Project Antigravity operates as a meritocratic community. Leadership is earned through contribution, knowledge sharing, and chapter stewardship.

## 4. Membership
Open to verified supply chain professionals. Membership levels (Core, Chapter Lead, Practitioner) are defined by activity and verification status.
