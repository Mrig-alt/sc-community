# VISION: The Supply Chain Practitioner Revolution

## The Why
Supply chain management has traditionally been split between high-level academic theory and fragmented, boots-on-the-ground execution. Existing communities are often gatekept by consulting firms or are too broad (general operations). Project Antigravity is the first community built **by practitioners, for practitioners**, leveraging modern tech to provide real-time, actionable intelligence.

## The "Supply Chain" Bet
We are doubling down on **Supply Chain** over general Operations because:
1. **Audience Energy**: Post-COVID, supply chain resilience is a global priority.
2. **Niche Fragmentation**: The sector is underserved by high-quality, tech-forward communities.
3. **Strategic Positioning**: Combining an MBA background with automation expertise allows for a unique "practitioner-first" value proposition.

## Core Objectives
- **Centralize Knowledge**: Create a global hub for supply chain professionals to share tools, tactics, and disruption management strategies.
- **Local Roots, Global Reach**: Scale through regional chapters (starting with India and Madrid) while maintaining a unified global standard.
- **Tech-Enabled Community**: Use Telegram bots and automation to lower the friction of participation and data sharing.

## The End Goal
To become the "Source of Truth" for supply chain practitioners—a community that is as professional and structured as a tech repository, yet as personal and responsive as a local network.
